Paper plane fold

Six minimal line icons that walk a sheet of paper through to a finished plane. Dashed lines are the next crease, solid lines are paper edges and folds already made.

The six steps

1 Blank sheet, first crease
2 Fold one corner in
3 Both corners make a nose
4 Fold in half
5 Fold the wings out
6 Ready to fly
